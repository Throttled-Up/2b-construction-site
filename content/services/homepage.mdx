## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a complex septic system license for my lakefront property in North Idaho?

If your lot has steep terrain, restrictive setbacks, or challenging soil conditions, Panhandle Health District will likely require an engineered septic design such as a pressure-distribution, mound, or sand-filter system. Only contractors holding an Idaho complex septic system license can legally design and install these systems. 2B Construction holds both simple and complex septic system licenses, so we can handle whatever your lot requires.
