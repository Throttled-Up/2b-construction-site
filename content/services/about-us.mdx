<ServiceSection slot="section_1" heading="Meet Tyler Beeman: 35+ Years of Civil Construction Experience">

Tyler Beeman founded 2B Construction LLC in 2021 in Athol, Idaho — not as a first venture, but after 35+ years managing large-scale civil construction projects. Tyler is a military veteran who spent decades overseeing complex civil infrastructure before bringing that project management discipline to residential septic and excavation work in North Idaho.

The move was intentional. Tyler saw homeowners on steep lakefront lots above Coeur d'Alene Lake and hillside properties near Hayden struggling to find contractors willing or able to do precision excavation work. He built 2B to fill that gap.

This is a four-person, family-owned operation. Tyler and his son work together on every project, from septic system installations to foundation excavation and site grading. When we show up on your property, you are working directly with the owner. No project manager relaying messages. Tyler runs the machine, reads the grade, and makes the calls.

</ServiceSection>

Tyler Beeman holds a **complex septic system license** in Idaho, qualifying 2B Construction to design and install:

- Mound systems
- Pressure distribution systems
- Other engineered configurations required on lakefront and hillside lots where Panhandle Health District mandates alternatives to conventional gravity systems

Many Idaho septic contractors are limited to conventional gravity installs and cannot legally perform the engineered work that properties along Coeur d'Alene Lake, Hayden Lake, and the Pend Oreille corridor routinely require.

<ServiceSection slot="section_3" heading="Why North Idaho Homeowners Choose 2B Construction">

We work across Kootenai County and Bonner County from our home base in Athol. Steep hillside lots above Coeur d'Alene Lake. Tight-access lakefront parcels on Hayden Lake. Rural properties along the Pend Oreille corridor where soil conditions demand engineered septic solutions. This is the terrain we built the company around.

Our excavator runs a **tilt rotator** — the bucket rotates 360 degrees and angles independently of the boom. On a steep lakefront lot with limited clearance between the house and the property line, that precision is the difference between a clean install and torn-up landscaping. Most competitors are not set up to work lots like these.

Concrete contractors in the area request 2B-prepped foundations specifically because of the grade accuracy we deliver on hillside sites. We install concrete septic tanks exclusively — they outlast plastic alternatives and hold up to North Idaho's freeze-thaw cycles.

If you moved here from somewhere with municipal sewer, whether you are replacing a 1960s lake cabin or adding an ADU, we handle Panhandle Health District permitting, soil evaluations, and aquifer-protection setback calculations so the process makes sense from day one.
